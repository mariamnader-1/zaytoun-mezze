Our story

Started on a rooftop, for twelve friends.

Zaytoun began as a rooftop dinner for twelve people and grew into a restaurant without changing how it cooks. Every mezze plate is still made the same day it's served, using recipes carried through three home kitchens before ours.

Nothing on the table arrives alone. Plates are meant to be passed, portions are meant to be shared, and the meal is meant to run long.
